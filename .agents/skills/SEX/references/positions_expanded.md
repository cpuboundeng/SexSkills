## Sitting & Kneeling

### Lap Seating (Face-to-Face)
**Setup**: The penetrating partner sits (on a chair, bed edge, or floor, legs out or cross-legged); the receiving partner straddles, facing them, legs wrapped around the waist. The cross-legged version is the **lotus position** — the Kama Sutra's classic.

**Sensation map**: Deep penetration with full body contact — chest to chest, arms around each other, faces inches apart. The receiving partner controls depth by sinking and rising; the penetrating partner can grip the hips and lift or guide. In the reverse lap version (back to chest), the receiving partner faces away — less intimacy, different angle, hands free to roam.

**What it expresses**: Face-to-face lap seating is the most *embracing* position — bodies completely wrapped, conversation-distance intimacy, the slow rock of it reads as devotion. The reverse version reads as use — the receiving partner's back, the penetrating partner's hands on hips and breasts.

**Writing angle**: Write the *closeness* — foreheads touching, breath mixing, arms locked, the slow deep rock. The dialogue is possible at this distance: whispered, half-moaned, intimate. Ideal for romantic and reconnection scenes; the reverse version for possessive or dominant registers.

### Chair / Edge Seating
**Setup**: The penetrating partner sits on the edge of a chair or bed; the receiving partner sits on top, feet on the floor, using their legs to drive the rhythm.

**Sensation map**: The receiving partner controls depth and tempo with their legs — bouncing, grinding, rocking. The penetrating partner's hands are free for the clit, breasts, hips. The angle can be adjusted by leaning back or forward.

**What it expresses**: The receiving partner's command of the encounter — active, athletic, present. The chair adds a slight taboo/office flavor; the edge-of-bed version is intimate and practical.

### Kneeling (Penetrating Partner)
**Setup**: The receiving partner lies on their back, knees raised; the penetrating partner kneels between their legs, hips elevated above.

**Sensation map**: The elevated angle changes the direction of thrust — deeper, with a different G-spot/prostate angle than flat missionary. The receiving partner's hips can be lifted onto pillows or the penetrating partner's knees for more depth. Full view of the receiving partner's body.

**Writing angle**: Write the *elevation* — the receiving partner looking down along their own body at the kneeling partner, the angle making everything more visible, more open. Good for dominant registers without losing face contact.

## Less Common & Acrobatic

These are the showpiece positions — visually striking, physically demanding, best deployed sparingly in fiction where the athleticism or novelty is the point.

### T-Square
**Setup**: The receiving partner lies on their back, knees up and apart; the penetrating partner lies perpendicular, hips under the arch of the receiver's legs.

**Sensation map**: The perpendicular angle gives a distinctive side-to-side stimulation pattern; the receiving partner's legs bracket the penetrating partner's waist. Eye contact is possible; a hand can reach the clit; the view of both partners is excellent.

**Writing angle**: The novelty is the detail — the perpendicular geometry, the different sensation axis. Write the adjustment, the discovery, the shared surprise at how it feels.

### Modified T-Square
**Setup**: The receiving partner's legs together, turned to one side; the penetrating partner kneels straight behind the hips.

**Sensation map**: Tighter, deeper, more controlled; the penetrating partner's hands on the hips steer everything.

### Piledriver
**Setup**: The receiving partner lies on their back, hips raised as high as possible (shoulders and neck supported by firm cushions), the penetrating partner standing, entering from above at a downward angle.

**Sensation map**: Extremely deep penetration; the body nearly folded. **Neck support is mandatory** — this position strains the neck and lower back and is genuinely difficult. The rusty bike pump variant reverses the angle with the receiver's bottom side up.

**Writing angle**: Write it as the advanced, porn-adjacent position it is — the folded body, the total exposure, the depth. The physical strain is part of the drama; the cushions and care are the competence detail. Not a first-date position.

### The Seventh Posture
**Setup**: The receiving partner lies on their side; the penetrating partner faces them, straddling the lower leg, lifting the upper leg onto shoulder or elbow.

**Sensation map**: Sometimes described as "for acrobats," but many find it comfortable — including during pregnancy. The lifted leg opens the angle; the position allows deep, angled thrusting without body weight on the receiver.

## Furniture & Environment

The surface shapes the scene. Write the surface and you've written the register:

| Surface | Scene Texture |
|---------|---------------|
| **Edge of the bed** | The receiving partner's legs dangling, the penetrating partner standing — casual height advantage, easy entry |
| **Table / desk** | The office scene — legs swept clear, the receiving partner laid back on paperwork; urgency and transgression |
| **Kitchen counter** | Domestic transgression — the mundane made erotic |
| **Chair / armchair** | Controlled, deliberate, the chair as a throne |
| **Ottoman / bench** | Bent over, presented — the body as offering |
| **Wall / shower** | Vertical urgency — caught, pinned, standing |
| **Floor / rug** | Raw, spontaneous — no furniture involved means no plan was involved |
| **Car** | Fumbling, constrained, adolescent heat — the constraint is the charm |
| **Stairs** | Awkward height variance, improvised angles — the comedy/heat of making it work |

**Writing angle**: The surface is a character with texture (cold wall, rough carpet, slippery counter) and meaning (the office desk says *risk*, the kitchen counter says *domestic heat*). Write the practical awkwardness honestly — the knee that hits the table, the foot that slips — then let the heat overtake the mechanics.

## Pregnancy Considerations

Late pregnancy sex is about comfort and protecting the belly; the positions that work are the ones that keep pressure off the abdomen:

- **Woman/partner on top** — full control of depth and pace; no pressure on the belly
- **Side-lying and spooning** — nothing presses on the belly; shallow, gentle; the late-pregnancy favorite
- **From behind** — the receiving partner supports the belly and breasts; deep but adjustable
- **Sitting/lap** — belly free, face-to-face intimacy

**Writing angle**: Pregnancy sex is underused in fiction and rich with emotional texture — the changed body, the tenderness of desire for it, the practical care that becomes part of the heat. The adjustments aren't obstacles; they're the intimacy.
