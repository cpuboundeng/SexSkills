## 69

**Setup**: Simultaneous oral sex — both partners giving and receiving at once. Variations: side-by-side (both lying on their sides, head-to-toe), one on top of the other, standing (one partner holding the other upside down — athletic).

**Sensation map**: Total simultaneity — every sensation doubled by the sensation of giving. The challenge is real: it's hard to focus on receiving while attending to giving. The *distraction* is the point — the breakdown of technique into mutual overwhelm.

**What it expresses**: Playful, generous, egalitarian — nobody is just serving or just being served. It reads as *mutual appetite*.

**Writing angle**: Write the *split attention* — the giver trying to keep their rhythm while their own pleasure keeps breaking their concentration, the sounds that escape into the other's body. The comedy and heat of both partners losing their composure at once. The moment one gives up and just *takes* their own pleasure is a delicious beat.

## Outercourse (Non-Penetrative Sex)

Non-penetrative sex excludes penetration — it's the entire universe of sex that isn't "intercourse": rubbing, grinding, manual play, mutual masturbation, and everything between. It's not "lesser" sex — it's often the sex people have when they can't or don't want penetration, and it can be the entire scene.

### Frottage & Dry Humping
Rubbing bodies (clothed or naked) against each other's genitals — the grinding of two bodies with friction as the point.
- **Dry humping**: clothed grinding; the heat and friction through fabric; the restraint of it
- **Grinding**: naked pelvic grinding; the clitoris against the other's body or pubic bone
- **Writing angle**: Write the *frustration and friction* — the heat building through layers, the hips finding rhythm, the moment clothing becomes unbearable. Dry humping is adolescent heat made adult; perfect for first-time scenes, slow-burn tension, or "we shouldn't but we will."

### Intercrural / Interfemoral Sex
The penis thrust between the partner's thighs — rubbing the vulva, scrotum, or perineum; often oiled. Historically common as a way to preserve virginity or avoid pregnancy.
- **Writing angle**: The intimacy of the thighs — the penetration-adjacent sensation without penetration; the specific tension of *almost*.

### Tribadism & Frot
Genital-genital rubbing between two people with vulvas (tribadism) or two people with penises (frot).
- **Tribadism**: grinding vulvas together, or one partner's vulva against the other's thigh, hip, or body; powerful clitoral stimulation; close, intense, face-to-face
- **Frot**: two penises rubbing together, shaft to shaft, often in a missionary-ish or scissored alignment
- **Writing angle**: The *alignment* is the technique — two bodies finding the angle that locks them together; the grinding rhythm; the mutual climax potential. Write the adjustment, the discovery, the "right there."

### Manual Sex
Hands on genitals — technique lives in the `fingering` and `masturbation` skills.
- **Handjob**: manual stimulation of the penis
- **Fingering**: manual stimulation of the vulva, clitoris, or anus
- **Mutual masturbation**: both partners touching themselves or each other, together
- **Writing angle**: Mutual masturbation is intimacy with walls down — write the *watching*: each partner witnessing the other's private pleasure, the vulnerability of being seen doing what's usually done alone, the hands that reach out to join.

### Mammary Intercourse & Footjobs
- **Mammary (breast) intercourse**: the penis between the breasts; visual and sensory; often paired with the mouth at the finish
- **Footjob**: the feet stimulate the genitals; tied to foot fetish territory (see the `bdsm` skill's foot worship reference)
- **Writing angle**: These read as *spectacle* — the body as instrument. Write the visual and the texture; they work best as a scene's garnish or a fetish's centerpiece, not the main course of a romantic scene.

### Intergluteal Sex
The penis between the buttocks without anal penetration — stimulation by the cleft, the tightness of the cheeks.
- **Writing angle**: The *almost* again — the heat of anal proximity without the act; great for tension scenes and teasing.

### Erotic Massage
Oiled hands working the body — technique-adjacent to sensuality itself. Slow, exploratory, the body mapped by touch before any focus on genitals.
- **Writing angle**: The massage as seduction — the incremental shift from therapeutic to erotic, the oil, the breath, the moment the hands stop being hands-on-a-back and become hands-on-a-person-wanting.
