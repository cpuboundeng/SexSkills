## Missionary

**Setup**: The receiving partner lies on their back, legs apart; the penetrating partner lies on top, face to face. The most common position on earth — and for good reason: full visibility, full body contact, and complete freedom to kiss.

**Sensation map**: Eye contact and kissing throughout; the penetrating partner's weight and warmth across the body. The receiving partner's hands are free to grip the penetrating partner's back, pull them closer, or hold their face. Depth can be shallow (grinding, teasing) or deep (thighs pushed up).

**Who controls**: Either — the penetrating partner sets rhythm from above, but the receiving partner can meet thrusts, tilt hips, wrap legs, or pull the penetrating partner down. This mutability is the position's genius.

**What it expresses**: Intimacy, equality, trust. Face-to-face is the most emotionally legible position — every reaction is visible. It reads as *connection* by default: romantic, tender, or intense-in-a-personal-way. It can be flipped into dominance (the receiving partner's wrists pinned, thighs forced apart) — the contrast lands because the default register is gentle.

**Variants**:
- **Legs on shoulders** — deeper penetration, tighter angle; the receiving partner's hips tilted up. Intense, exposing, more demanding.
- **Butterfly** — legs lifted toward the ceiling, resting against the penetrating partner; deep, open, almost suspended.
- **Coital alignment** — the penetrating partner shifts up so the base of their shaft presses the clitoris; shallow thrusts with grinding pressure. The "best for clitoral stimulation" variant — orgasm without velocity.
- **Ankles-to-ears / Viennese oyster** — legs folded to the ears; the deepest, most folded missionary variant. Extreme depth and exposure; for flexible bodies.

**Writing angle**: Write the *faces* — the micro-expressions, the eye contact that holds, the kiss that breaks mid-movement because neither can pull away. The receiving partner's hands on the penetrating partner's back or face are the emotional detail. For intensity: write the shift into legs-on-shoulders, the angle change that makes the receiving partner gasp.

## Doggy Style

**Setup**: The receiving partner on all fours; the penetrating partner enters from behind. The most primal of the classics — the face is hidden, the body is presented.

**Sensation map**: Deep penetration; the angle targets the front wall (G-spot in people with vaginas; the prostate in people with prostates) with every thrust. The penetrating partner controls depth and rhythm completely and has a full view of the body — the arch of the back, the curve of the ass, the flex of the thighs. The receiving partner's hands can brace, clutch the sheets, or hold themselves up.

**Who controls**: The penetrating partner, almost entirely. The receiving partner can angle their hips to change depth and pressure — dropping the chest for deeper, arching more for shallower, tilting to steer the angle.

**What it expresses**: Power, primality, possession. Face hidden, presented — it reads as raw and dominated by default, though it can be tender (the penetrating partner leaning over, whispering, reaching around to touch). The visual line of the body is the erotic geometry: spine to ass to the join of the bodies.

**Variants**:
- **Prone bone** — the receiving partner lies flat, the penetrating partner on top; shallower, tighter, grinding. Intimate and intense; the weight and the hidden face make it almost secretive.
- **Leapfrog** — torso angled down, hips up; maximum depth, maximum exposure.
- **Downward angle** — the penetrating partner rises on their knees, hands on the receiving partner's back; a steeper angle that changes the sensation completely.
- **Rear kneeling with arms pulled back** — the receiving partner kneels upright while the penetrating partner gently pulls their arms back at the wrists; exposed, controlled, slightly suspended.

**Writing angle**: Write the *presentation* — the receiving partner's body offering itself, the arch deepening as arousal rises, the penetrating partner's hands on hips steering. For tenderness: the lean-over, the whisper against the back of the neck, the hand sliding around to find the clit — the gentleness inside the primal frame.

## Cowgirl & Reverse Cowgirl

**Setup**: The penetrating partner lies on their back; the receiving partner straddles on top. Facing each other in cowgirl; facing away in reverse cowgirl.

**Sensation map**: The receiving partner has **full control** of depth, speed, and angle — they can grind, ride, rock, or hover. For people with prostates, leaning back and rocking levers the tip against the P-spot. The penetrating partner's hands are free — on hips, breasts, thighs, or the small of the back.

**Who controls**: The receiving partner — entirely. That's the position's whole point and its erotic content: the power flip. The penetrating partner's experience is being ridden, watched, *used* — which is precisely the appeal.

**What it expresses**: The receiving partner's agency and desire; the penetrating partner's surrender to being wanted. Cowgirl reads as *taking what you want*; reverse cowgirl reads as *using what you want* — the reverse variant is more impersonal, more about the ride than the connection.

**Variants**:

- **Arch back** — hands on the penetrating partner's thighs or the floor behind; open, exposed, different angle.
- **Knees together** — legs folded close; tighter channel, grinding contact.
- **Lateral coital position** — the receiving partner rolled slightly to the side, pelvis on top, weight beside; recommended by Masters and Johnson, preferred by most of their study participants; low effort, high contact, easy clitoral access.

**Writing angle**: Write the *reins* — the receiving partner setting the pace, the penetrating partner's hands finding places to hold, the whole dynamic of watching and being ridden. The visual from below is underused in fiction: the silhouette, the rhythm of the ride, the face lost in pleasure. For reverse cowgirl: the anonymity, the hands on hips, the view of the back and ass — write the distance it creates.

## Spooning

**Setup**: Both partners on their sides, the penetrating partner behind, bodies nested like spoons. The receiving partner's top leg raised or hooked back over the penetrating partner's hip.

**Sensation map**: Shallow-to-moderate penetration at a gentle angle; the bodies are fully nested — the penetrating partner's chest against the receiving partner's back, a hand on the hip, breast, or clit. Minimal effort, maximum contact. The angle targets the back wall of the vagina; with the top leg raised higher, the angle shifts and deepens.

**Who controls**: Shared, gently. The receiving partner controls initial depth by pressing back; the penetrating partner controls rhythm from behind. It's the position of mutual laziness — and mutual tenderness.

**What it expresses**: Intimacy without confrontation — closeness with faces hidden. It's the position of comfort, of quiet, of the middle of the night. It reads as *safety*: two people wrapped together, no one performing. Late-pregnancy favorite because nothing presses on the belly.

**Writing angle**: Write the *nested* quality — the breath against the back of the neck, the hands that wander without asking, the half-awake arousal of a partner reached for in the dark. The dialogue is whispered, half into pillows. It's the position for scenes about *being together* more than *being fucked* — the domestic erotica of the morning, the middle of the night, the quiet reconnection.

## Side-Lying (Face-to-Face)

**Setup**: Both partners on their sides, facing each other; the receiving partner's top leg over the penetrating partner's hip or hooked on their waist.

**Sensation map**: Shallow-to-moderate depth at a comfortable angle; full eye contact and kissing throughout; hands free to roam. One of the most comfortable positions for sustained, slow sex — nothing supports weight, everything supports touch.

**Who controls**: Shared — rhythm can come from either; the hooked leg is the accelerator.

**What it expresses**: Conversation — the position of talking-while-fucking, of lazy Saturday intimacy, of two people looking at each other and taking their time. It reads as *partnership*: neither on top, neither under, bodies level.

**Writing angle**: Write the *eye contact* and the *talk* — the half-sentences, the smiles, the watching each other's faces as the rhythm builds. The physical detail: the top leg hooked over the hip, the foot running down the calf, the hands that can reach anywhere without moving far. Ideal for romantic, playful, or post-conflict-reconciliation scenes — the intimacy of faces at the same height.

## Standing

**Setup**: Both partners standing, facing each other; the receiving partner's back against a wall (or the penetrating partner lifting them). In the lifted variant (the Kama Sutra's Suspended Congress; called Ekiben in Japan), the receiving partner wraps legs around the penetrating partner's waist and arms around their neck.

**Sensation map**: Depth and angle vary with height differences — the shorter partner can stand on a stair, wear heels, or be lifted. Wall support enables solid thrusting; the lifted variant gives deep penetration with near-total body contact, but requires strength and a strong frame. Legs around the waist open the hips wide; the angle is often more direct.

**Who controls**: The penetrating partner — the standing position is typically urgent and vertical; there's no lying down, no settling in. It's the position of *not making it to the bed*.

**What it expresses**: Urgency, heat, spontaneity — the bodies couldn't wait. Wall-standing reads as caught, claimed, pinned; the lifted variant reads as *possessed* — the receiving partner held, suspended, entirely supported by the penetrating partner's body.

**Writing angle**: Write the *immediacy* — clothes half-off, the wall cold against the back, the legs that have to wrap to hold on. The lifted variant is the payoff of strength and desire: the receiving partner's weight held, the grunt of effort, the vulnerability of being completely off the ground. Perfect for adventure sex, make-up-for-lost-time sex, angry sex — anything urgent.
