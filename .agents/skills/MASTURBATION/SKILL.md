# MASTURBATION

## Overview

Masturbation is the most intimate, honest form of sex — a character alone with their own body, their own desires, their own rhythm. In smut and erotic fiction, a well-written masturbation scene reveals vulnerability, self-knowledge, and raw desire. It's the only sex act where both performer and audience are the same person. ## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **One-note technique.** Real masturbation varies — speed, pressure, grip change over time. Show the evolution.
- **Ignoring the mind.** A masturbation scene is 50% physical, 50% psychological. What is the character thinking?
- **Too clinical.** Don't list anatomy like a textbook. Weave details naturally into the action.
- **The finish too fast.** The most erotic masturbation scenes stretch time. Show the build. The orgasm is just the payoff.
- **No aftermath.** The 30 seconds after climax carry emotional weight.
- **Forgetting lubrication.** Wetness matters — for comfort, for sound, for the visual.
- **Treating it as less than "real" sex.** It's real. It's intimate. It deserves the same care.
- **Not matching technique to body type.** Sensitive clit needs gentler touch. Foreskinned vs. circumcised matters. Know your character's body.

## POV and Interiority

Masturbation is the most interior sex scene. The character is alone — no partner to react to, no dialogue to carry the scene. This means:

- **Lean into internal monologue.** What is the character thinking? Memories? Fantasies? Observations about their own body?
- **Use the body as a mirror for emotion.** A character who is lonely will touch differently than one who is lonely *and* angry. A character who is confident will explore differently than one who feels shame.
- **Let the rhythm of the prose match the rhythm of the hand.** Short, staccato sentences during the urgent, fast strokes. Longer, flowing sentences during the slow, deliberate moments.
- **Include memories and fantasies.** A character touching themselves often drifts — into memory, into fantasy, into a blend of both. The physical touch triggers the mental wandering, and the mental wandering changes the physical sensation.
