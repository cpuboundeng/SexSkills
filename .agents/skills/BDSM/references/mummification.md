# Mummification Guide

This reference covers wrapping methods, the sensory experience, and safety for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

Mummification wraps the body until it can barely move — complete immobility wrapped in a sensory cocoon. The bound person can feel everything and move nothing; sound is muffled, warmth builds, and the world shrinks to sensation and breath. It is immobilization at its most total, often paired with hoods, vibrators, impact, or just the long, quiet surrender of being wrapped.

## Methods

| Method | Description | Intensity |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Plastic wrap** | Layered cling film around the body | Medium — tight, restrictive, slick |
| **Bandage wrap** | Elastic/medical bandages wrapped repeatedly | Medium-Hard — conforming, soft |
| **Bondage tape** | Self-adhesive vinyl that sticks to itself, not skin/hair | Medium — easy, no stickiness |
| **Vet wrap / cohesive bandage** | Stretchy, self-adhering | Medium — forgiving, breathable |
| **Sleep sack** | Canvas/leather/neoprene sack, body pulled in | Hard — structured, heavy |
| **Body bag** | Full encasement bag with zipper and hood | Hard — total enclosure |
| **Shrink wrap / vacuum bed** | Plastic sealed and air removed | Hard — total pressure, advanced |

**Wrapping order matters**: limbs first, torso later; the head is only wrapped if breathing is fully secured (see safety). Never wrap so tightly that the chest can't expand.

## The Sensory Experience

- **Touch**: constant, even pressure — like being held by the whole world; inability to shift becomes its own sensation
- **Sound**: muffled; the bottom's own breathing and heartbeat become loud — the body's soundtrack
- **Sight**: darkness or dimness if the head is wrapped/hooded
- **Smell**: the material (plastic, leather, neoprene) and one's own scent concentrated
- **Internal**: muscle fatigue from stillness, building warmth, the growing urge to move that cannot be answered
- **The psychological**: total surrender — nothing to do, nowhere to go, no choices at all. For some this is bliss; for others it's claustrophobia. Know the bottom.

## Safety

- **Breathing first**: airway never covered unless a breathing tube is secured and tested; test breathing before completing any wrap
- **Temperature**: the body heats fast under layers — monitor for overheating, sweating, and distress; strip layers if needed
- **Circulation**: wrap must restrict movement without cutting off blood flow; check fingers/toes for color and sensation
- **Nerve pressure**: watch for tingling/numbness; reposition as needed
- **Claustrophobia**: panic can hit even without a history of it — the ability to signal and be released instantly is non-negotiable
- **Time limit**: 30–45 minutes max for full mummification, less for first-timers
- **Never alone**: a mummified person cannot self-rescue — constant monitoring, always
- **EMT shears**: in hand before the first wrap; cutting the wrap out is normal
- **Aftercare**: skin is sensitive after pressure; unwrap slowly, gentle massage, cool room, water, emotional grounding

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Dangerous | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Wrapping the head casually | Airway obstruction | Head wrap only with secured breathing |
| Too tight over the chest | Breathing restricted | Test full deep breath mid-wrap |
| Heat buildup ignored | Overheating, heat exhaustion | Monitor, strip layers, time limits |
| No quick release | Panic or emergency unanswered | Shears in hand; cut freely |
| First-timer full wrap | Claustrophobia overwhelms | Start with partial wraps |
| Untended | Cannot self-rescue | Never alone, ever |

## Writing Tips

- Mummification writes beautifully from the bottom's interiority: the world shrinking to breath, heartbeat, and the Dom's voice through the layers
- The pressure is the sensation: describe the constant, even weight, the warmth building, the body settling into stillness
- The muffled world is a sound palette: breathing, heartbeat, fabric rustling, the Dom's voice filtered through layers
- Unwrapping is intimacy: the slow reveal of skin, the relief of movement returning, the marks the pressure left
