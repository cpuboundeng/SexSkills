# Exhibitionism & Voyeurism Guide

This reference covers exhibitionism, voyeurism, public play, and their legal and consent dimensions for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

- **Exhibitionism**: arousal from being seen — by a partner, a group, or strangers
- **Voyeurism**: arousal from watching — a partner, a couple, or others
- Many people enjoy both — the watcher and the watched are two halves of the same desire for exposure and witness

In BDSM, exhibitionism becomes power play: the Dom displaying the sub like property, ordering exposure, controlling who sees and when. Voyeurism becomes control too: the Dom watching the sub perform, or the sub watching the Dom with another. The audience — real, invited, or implied — becomes part of the scene.

## Exhibitionism Types

| Type | Description | Risk |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Private exhibition** | Nude or partial at home, curtains open | Low |
| **Semi-public** | Nude near a window; neighbors might see | Medium |
| **Balcony/window** | Standing naked at an open window or balcony | Medium-High |
| **Club exhibition** | Nude or partial in a sex-positive venue | Medium (venue rules) |
| **Public exhibition** | Nude in a public space | High (legal) |
| **Digital exhibition** | Streaming, webcam, recorded for an audience | Variable (permanent!) |
| **Group exhibition** | Being seen by multiple people | Variable |
| **"Caught" play** | The fantasy of being discovered — staged or real | Variable |

## Voyeurism Types

| Type | Description | Risk |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Watching at home** | Partner performs naked or sexually | Low |
| **Club watching** | Observing in a sex-positive space | Low-Medium |
| **Watching a partner with another** | Cuckold/cuckquean dynamics | Medium (emotional) |
| **Live streaming** | Watching remotely | Variable |
| **Recording** | Filming for private or shared viewing | Variable (consent!) |
| **Strangers in public** | Observing in appropriate contexts | Legal caution |

## Public Play Considerations

| Factor | What to Consider |
|--------|------------------|
| **The law** | Exposure laws vary wildly by jurisdiction — research; "it was a fantasy" is not a defense |
| **Consent of the audience** | Unaware strangers cannot consent to being part of a scene — public kink is legally and ethically different from club kink |
| **Identification risk** | Can the participants be recognized? What are the real-world stakes? |
| **Escape route** | How fast can the scene end if someone approaches? |
| **Anonymity** | Face covered? Clothing strategy? |
| **Clothing strategy** | Barely-there vs. fully covered with hidden exposure |
| **Digital permanence** | Photos/video last forever; distribution is uncontrollable |
| **Venue rules** | Sex-positive venues have explicit rules — follow them |

## Scene Ideas — Exhibitionism

- Sub at an open window in lingerie while the Dom watches from inside
- Sub blindfolded in a park; the Dom describes being watched (fantasy, not actual risk)
- Sub wears a remote toy under clothes all day; the Dom controls it (see `vibrator_bondage.md`)
- Sub poses nude for the Dom's camera in private

## Scene Ideas — Voyeurism

- The sub watches the Dom with another from a nearby room
- The sub watches their partner undress through a peephole (with consent)
- The sub is blindfolded and told they're being watched — the *belief* is the scene
- The sub performs for a camera while the Dom watches from another room
- The Dom narrates the sub's body while the sub watches themselves in a mirror

## Safety & Ethics

- **Know the law**: public exposure and non-consensual recording are crimes in most places — research before any scene
- **Audience consent**: the people who see the scene are not props; public play that involves unwilling strangers is a violation, not kink
- **Photo/video consent**: who owns the recordings, where they live, who may see them — agreed in writing if necessary
- **Anonymity**: decide before play whether faces will be visible; even "private" photos can leak
- **The fantasy distinction**: much exhibitionism is best played as *implied* exposure — the thrill of "someone might see" without actually involving strangers
- **Emotional risk**: exhibitionism can leave people feeling exposed or exhilarated — sometimes both in the same session; aftercare matters
- **Check-ins**: even in a scene with an audience, the sub needs a signal and the ability to stop
- **Aftercare**: the post-scene debrief for exhibition includes the *who might have seen* conversation — settle it honestly

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Involving unwilling strangers | It's a crime and a violation | Play implied exposure; use venues |
| Recording without full consent | Betrayal, legal exposure | Explicit recording consent, always |
| Assuming "private" stays private | Digital permanence | Assume nothing digital is private |
| Ignoring local law | Real criminal consequences | Research before any scene |
| No escape plan | Panic, discovery, danger | Exit routes, clothing strategy |
| Skipping the debrief | Shame or fear goes unaddressed | Talk through who might have seen |

## Writing Tips

- Exhibition scenes run on the *gaze*: describe being seen — the imagined watcher, the window, the door ajar, the camera's eye
- The exposure is the sensation: the air on bare skin, the thrill of the boundary, the flush of being looked at
- Voyeurism is about framing: the door cracked, the mirror, the screen — the watcher's hunger is the scene
- The implied audience is often hotter than the real one: "someone might be watching" does more work than a full crowd
- Write the aftermath honestly: the rush, the shame-tinged thrill, the Dom's reassurance that they were beautiful, wanted, seen
