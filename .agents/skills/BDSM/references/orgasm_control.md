# Orgasm Control Guide

This reference covers edging, denial, forced release, and the authority structure of orgasm control for the BDSM skill. (This guide absorbs the former `edging_denial.md`.)

## What It Is

Orgasm control puts the orgasm itself under the Dominant's authority: when it happens, whether it happens, how it happens. It's one of the most purely *psychological* kinks in BDSM — no implements required, just the bottom's own body and the Dom's word. The frustration builds, the sensitivity compounds, and the release (when granted) becomes the most intense reward available. The power is total: the bottom literally cannot come without permission.

## Orgasm Control Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Edging** | Brought to the edge and pulled back, repeatedly — the classic |
| **Denial** | No orgasm at all for the scene, the day, or longer |
| **Granted release** | The bottom must ask; the Dom decides when and how |
| **Forced release** | Orgasm driven past the point of wanting — intensity as surrender |
| **Delayed release** | Held at the edge for a set time before permission |
| **Multiple control** | How many times, and under what conditions |
| **Nocturnal control** | Permission granted/withheld across days — text or call at the Dom's discretion |
| **Conditioned denial** | A task or behavior must be completed before release is earned |

## Edging Techniques

| Technique | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Stop-start** | Bring to the edge, stop all stimulation, let it settle just below the edge, repeat |
| **Edge and impact** | Impact as the bottom nears the edge — the strike pulls them back |
| **Breath edge** | A breath-hold or breath change at the edge — see `breath_play.md` for risk |
| **Position edge** | A difficult position while edging; a leg shake or droop is a "fail" |
| **Verbal edge** | The Dom talks the bottom up, then talks them down — voice as the dial |
| **Edge with restraint** | Bound, unable to adjust; the Dom controls all stimulation |

## Denial Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Scene denial** | No release during the scene or for a set period |
| **Permission-based** | Must ask; the Dom decides, sometimes mid-question |
| **Tease and deny** | Edge repeatedly, stop, repeat — the cycle is the point |
| **Cum trap** | Brought to the edge again and again, told yes — then taken away |
| **Orgasm lottery** | A reward is earned; the Dom rolls dice or sets arbitrary rules |
| **Long-term denial** | Days or weeks; the release becomes a milestone |
| **Conditioned denial** | A task, a report, a ritual earned before release is considered |
| **Ruined orgasm** | Release without satisfaction — the bottom crests and falls without the peak; exquisite cruelty |

## Scene Arc

1. **Establish the rules** — who controls, when the bottom may ask, what counts as an edge, what "fail" means
2. **Build** — stimulation with clear signals; the bottom knows they're approaching
3. **The edge** — the brink; then the stop, the hold, the settling
4. **Repeat** — each cycle compounds sensitivity and frustration
5. **The decision** — grant or deny; the Dom's choice is the scene's climax
6. **The release (if granted)** — under the Dom's exact conditions: position, speed, duration
7. **The aftermath** — sweet, punishing, or worshipful depending on the dynamic
8. **Aftercare** — denial leaves emotional residue; debrief and reassurance

## What It Feels Like

- **The edge**: the whole body gathering toward a point — trembling, breath hitching, the mind gone
- **The stop**: a wave of frustration and relief at once — the body denied what it was reaching for
- **The compounding**: each cycle makes the next edge higher, the sensitivity sharper
- **The granted release**: after denial, the orgasm is enormous — the body's gratitude, the surrender to the voice that permitted it
- **The ruined orgasm**: the crest without the fall — a hollow pulse, a desperate ache, total submission

## Safety & Aftercare

- **Frustration is real**: denial can produce irritability, tears, and genuine distress — these are not failures, they're the intensity
- **Physical discomfort**: prolonged edging can ache — the "blue balls/ovaries" phenomenon; check in about actual pain
- **The release valve**: the bottom needs a way to signal "I need release" distinct from "stop everything" — a yellow that means "please let me come" rather than "end the scene"
- **Communication**: the Dom must distinguish bratting from distress, teasing from genuine need
- **Hydration and fuel**: orgasm control scenes are physically and emotionally draining
- **Aftercare**: post-release bottoms can feel depleted, vulnerable, or euphoric — or crash days later if denied long-term; scheduled check-ins matter
- **Not for everyone**: some find denial thrilling; others find it genuinely distressing — know the bottom, renegotiate freely

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| No clear rules | Confusion and resentment mid-scene | Define edges, fails, and asking protocol |
| Ignoring real distress | Frustration becomes pain | Separate "need release" from "stop" signals |
| Long-term denial without check-ins | Emotional crash, physical ache | Scheduled check-ins, flexibility |
| Punishing the bottom for reacting | Reactions are the point | The reaction IS the scene |
| Skipping aftercare | Denial residue unaddressed | Debrief, reassurance, care |

## Writing Tips

- The Dom's voice is the instrument: the command to hold, the permission to come, the word that breaks the bottom open
- The edge is a place to live: describe the trembling, the pleading, the body gathered at the brink — and the cruel gentleness of the pull-back
- The stopped orgasm is its own sensation: the wave that crests and recedes, the ache it leaves
- The granted release after denial is one of the most powerful moments in BDSM fiction: the bottom's gratitude, the wreck of their body, the Dom's satisfaction
- Rules and rituals (counting edges, earning permission) make the authority visible on the page
