# Collaring Guide

This reference covers collar types, ceremonies, symbolism, and daily wear for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

A collar is the physical symbol of a D/s bond — ownership, commitment, and belonging made wearable. It can be a scene prop, a daily reminder, or a deeply meaningful milestone on par with an engagement ring. The act of collaring — choosing the collar, the ceremony, the moment it clicks around the throat — is one of the most emotionally charged rituals in BDSM. The collar says what words struggle to: *you are mine, and I am yours*.

## Types of Collars

| Type | Description | Context |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Leather collar** | Classic wide leather, buckle or D-ring | The standard; versatile, durable |
| **Pleather/faux** | Softer, affordable | Scenes, beginners |
| **Metal collar** | Steel, iron, brass | Harder edge; industrial feel |
| **Choker** | Thin, sleek, with or without pendant | Subtle, daily wear |
| **Dog-style collar** | Wider, buckle, tag | Pet play; ownership symbol |
| **Tagged collar** | Name or symbol engraved | Identification, ownership |
| **Custom collar** | Engraved, personalized | Permanent relationship |
| **Collar + chain** | Collar attached to a lead | The Dom holds physical control |
| **Day collar** | Discreet jewelry (chain, ring, clasp) | 24/7 dynamics; workplace-safe |

## The Collaring Ceremony

1. **The choosing** — the collar selected with the sub in mind; sometimes the sub chooses, sometimes the Dom, sometimes together
2. **The preparation** — the space, the ritual; often kneeling, sometimes candles, often witnesses
3. **The words** — the Dom speaks the commitment: what the collar means, what is promised, what is expected
4. **The kneeling** — the sub presents their throat
5. **The collaring** — the collar placed, the buckle clicked, the lock fastened — the moment of transformation
6. **The first command** — the new dynamic takes effect immediately: "Rise, and come with me"
7. **The seal** — a kiss, a touch, a mark

## Collaring in Daily Life

- **The collar as switch**: the collar going on/off marks the boundary of the dynamic
- **Day collars**: discreet pieces that carry meaning the world can't read
- **The removal**: who may remove it, when, and what removal means (punishment, scene end, or end of the dynamic?) — this is negotiated
- **The collar as constant**: in 24/7 dynamics, the collar's weight is a continuous reminder of belonging

## Safety & Considerations

- **Fit**: snug but comfortable — two fingers between collar and throat; allow for swelling after scenes or in heat
- **Material**: leather needs breaking in; metal can be heavy and cold; check for irritation
- **Collar rash**: real — rotate wear, keep the skin clean and dry, take breaks
- **Choking caution**: a collar is decorative restraint, not a choking device — never use a collar as a leash with force; see `breath_play.md`
- **Meaning**: never assume what a collar means — for one sub it's a weekend prop, for another a marriage; negotiate its significance
- **Removal rights**: how and when the collar comes off is part of the negotiation — unilaterally removing a collar can break a bond
- **Aftercare**: collaring is deeply emotional; the newly collared may experience euphoria, tears, or vulnerability — treat the moment with care

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Collaring casually | Cheapens a meaningful ritual | Reserve it; make it matter |
| Assuming collar = slave | Meaning varies wildly | Negotiate the symbolism |
| Too-tight collar | Choking, marks, panic | Fit check, always |
| Using the collar as a choke point | Genuinely dangerous | Collar is symbolic restraint, not a tool |
| No discussion of removal | Power imbalance in the wrong place | Removal rules are part of the bond |

## Writing Tips

- The collaring ceremony is peak emotional material: the kneeling, the words, the click of the buckle — write it with full weight
- The collar is a constant sensory presence: its weight on the throat, its warmth from the skin, the tug of the chain, the sound of the D-ring
- The collar changes how the sub moves and feels: the chin lifted by the leather, the hand that reaches up to touch it
- Show what the collar means through small moments: the sub touching it during the day, the Dom's finger under the band, the removal at scene's end
