## Control Dynamics

- **The remote**: the Dom controls on/off and intensity from anywhere — the bottom can't predict when the buzzing starts

## Safety

- **Vibration numbness**: prolonged vibration desensitizes — take breaks, move the vibrator, dial down
- **Heat**: motors heat up; check the casing against your own skin regularly
- **Battery life**: never trap someone with a dying vibrator — the "don't stop now" panic is real; have backups
- **Lube compatibility**: water-based for silicone toys; never silicone lube on silicone toys
- **Removal plan**: vibrators must be reachable or quickly removable in an emergency — no knots near the toy
- **Internal toys**: flared bases or retrieval rings, always; never internal toys in bondage without them
- **Skin check**: prolonged vibration can irritate skin; check the contact point after sessions
