# Pillory Play Guide

This reference covers pillory types, immobilization, exposure, and safety for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

Pillory play restrains the sub in a fixed frame that immobilizes and exposes them — head, wrists, and often ankles held in place, the body on display. Borrowed from the historical punishment stocks, the pillory in kink transforms public shame into erotic exposure: the sub cannot move, cannot hide, cannot do anything but be seen. The vulnerability is architectural — the frame holds the body, and the body has no choice but to present itself.

## Types of Pillories

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Neck pillory** | Frame with a neck hole; hands free or bound |
| **Hand pillory** | Wall or floor-mounted wrist restraints |
| **Full-body pillory** | Head, wrists, and ankles all secured |
| **Stockade** | Classic stocks — wrists and neck in a folding frame |
| **Surrey** | Wooden horse with pegs; sub straddles, ankles/wrists secured |
| **Cucking stool** | Chair with head and hand holes; seated, exposed |
| **Improvised** | Bedposts, wall anchors, door restraints as makeshift pillories |

## The Experience

- **Exposure**: the core of it — immobilized and visible; nothing to do but be seen
- **Waiting**: extended stillness builds anticipation; time slows; the mind turns inward
- **Forced observation**: others may watch, touch, or interact — the sub can't refuse or retreat
- **Sound amplification**: breathing, movement attempts, and involuntary sounds become loud in the stillness
- **The psychological**: being "put away" in a frame — treated as an object of display — is a deep surrender
- **The body's dialogue**: unable to move, the sub's responses become internal — flushed skin, quickened breath, the slight tremor the frame lets show

## Scene Ideas

- Neck pillory in the corner; the Dom ignores, uses, or visits the sub at will
- Wall-mounted wrist restraints with blindfold; impact or teasing from unpredictable angles
- Surrey with the bottom exposed; caning or flogging at leisure
- Full-body restraint; the Dom feeds and waters the sub only on their schedule
- Public pillory: the sub displayed where others can see (with full consent)
- The pillory as punishment: corner time made architectural — the sub left in the frame to think

## Safety

- **Nerve and circulation checks**: wrists, neck, and ankles are all pressure zones — check regularly for tingling, color change, numbness
- **Fit**: holes must be large enough to avoid pressure on the throat's sides; the neck hole never tight
- **Time limits**: extended immobility causes cramping and circulation issues — 20–30 minutes max for full restraint, less for first-timers
- **The neck rule**: a neck hole that's too tight, or a sub that slumps, is a strangulation risk — the frame must support, not compress
- **Emergency release**: quick-release mechanisms must be reachable by the Dom at all times
- **Position fatigue**: standing or straddling positions strain muscles — supports, breaks, monitoring
- **Hydration**: the sub can't reach water — provide via straw or cup
- **Never alone**: the frame is total immobilization — constant monitoring, always
- **Aftercare**: stiffness, soreness, and emotional vulnerability are common after long immobilization — massage, warmth, water, grounding

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Dangerous | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Tight neck hole | Choking, panic | Fit check; support, never compress |
| Long sessions unmonitored | Circulation, cramping, faintness | Time limits, regular checks |
| No quick release | Panic or emergency unanswered | Release accessible, always |
| Slumping sub in neck frame | Airway risk | Watch posture; adjust the frame |
| Untended | Cannot self-rescue | Never alone, ever |

## Writing Tips

- The pillory is a stage: describe the sub's exposure, the Dom's leisurely use of the immobile body, the audience (real or implied)
- The stillness is the sensation: the sub's world reduced to breath, skin, and the sounds around them
- The frame is a character: the wood's creak, the grain against skin, the weight of the lock
- Waiting is erotic time: write the sub's interiority during the long minutes — the arousal of being forgotten, the ache of being remembered
