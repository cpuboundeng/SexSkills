# Dominance & Submission Guide

This reference covers dominant/submissive archetypes, protocols, rituals, discipline, and power exchange levels for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

D/s is the deliberate structuring of a scene or relationship around power exchange: one person (the Dominant) takes control, the other (the submissive) yields it. Unlike impact play or bondage, the implements are optional — the exchange itself is the kink. The sub gives up decision-making and receives safety, structure, and direction; the Dom takes responsibility and receives devotion, surrender, and visible trust. The erotic charge lives in the constant, felt weight of *who belongs to whom*.

## Protocols & Rituals

Protocols are the rules that make the power exchange *visible* — the small behaviors that constantly reassert who holds the power:

- Kneeling on the Dom's return
- Forms of address (Sir, Mistress, Master, Daddy/Mommy)
- Eye contact rules (maintain or avert, per dynamic)
- Speech rules (when/how to speak, permission to speak)
- Position rules (where the sub sits, stands, kneels in a room)
- Clothing rules (collar at home, underwear chosen by Dom)
- Morning/evening rituals (serving coffee, reporting the day)
- Gifting as devotion tokens
- Regular check-ins (daily texts, reports)

The power of protocol: it turns ordinary life into constant, quiet kink. A text at 3pm — "Report." — carries the whole dynamic in one word.

## Discipline

Discipline is the enforcement of rules with consequences — not punishment for its own sake, but the structure that makes rules real.

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Behavioral** | Posture, speech, eye contact, punctuality |
| **Physical** | Positions, exercise, grooming standards |
| **Task-based** | Chores, journaling, lines, daily reports |
| **Protocol** | Formalized speech, rituals, forms of address |
| **Educational** | Learning skills, demonstrating knowledge |
| **Punishment** | Spanking, deprivation, extra tasks, corner time |

### Punishment vs. Funishment
- **Punishment** corrects genuine rule-breaking: spanking, corner time, writing lines, privilege loss. Calm, consistent, agreed-upon consequences.
- **Funishment** is play that looks like punishment (bratting that earns a spanking) — mutually enjoyed, stops when either safewords.
- The distinction matters: a sub who broke a *real* rule being "punished" with something they love is a broken dynamic. Negotiate which is which.

## Power Exchange Levels

1. **Scene-only** — the exchange lives inside a scene; daily life is vanilla
2. **Situational** — certain activities or times carry the dynamic (bedroom only, weekends)
3. **24/7 dynamic** — continuous power exchange in daily life, within negotiated limits
4. **TPE (Total Power Exchange)** — the Dom controls nearly all aspects of the sub's life; the deepest, most demanding level — requires exceptional trust and negotiation

## What It Feels Like

- **The sub**: relief — the freedom of not deciding; the safety of belonging; the ache of waiting for approval; the pride of being praised; the surrender of being "kept"
- **The Dom**: responsibility that weighs good; the arousal of being obeyed; the tenderness of holding someone's trust completely; the quiet pride of watching the sub bloom under their care
- **In subspace/dynamic flow**: the sub's world narrows to the Dom's voice; the Dom's world narrows to the sub's response

## Scene Arc (Protocol Scene)

1. **Negotiation** — rules for this scene, safeword, what submission looks like today
2. **The opening ritual** — kneeling, address, the Dom's acknowledgment of the sub
3. **The exchange** — commands, service, play; the Dom directing, the sub yielding
4. **The peak** — the most intense surrender: orgasm control, impact, worship
5. **Praise and grounding** — the Dom reasserting care: "You did so well. You're mine."
6. **Aftercare** — physical care, emotional re-entry, equal footing restored
7. **Debrief** — what worked, what to adjust

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Skipping negotiation in long-term dynamics | Assumptions accumulate; resentments grow | Keep renegotiating; dynamics evolve |
| Dom reading only from a script | Feels performative, not real | Follow the sub's actual responses |
| Punishment for things never agreed on | Unfairness destroys trust | Rules are negotiated; consequences are known |
| Sub faking enjoyment | Erosion of honesty; eventual crash | Create safety for real feedback |
| No check-ins | Small issues become big ones | Scheduled and spontaneous check-ins |
| 24/7 without framework | Burnout, blurring of consent | Limits, safewords, and off-ramps apply 24/7 too |
| The Dom never receiving care | Doms drop too | Doms need aftercare and support |

## Writing Tips

- The hottest D/s writing shows power in *small moments*: the word "kneel" said casually; the sub's body obeying before their mind catches up; a hand tilting a chin up; praise that lands like a reward
- Interiority is everything: the sub's internal monologue of surrender, the Dom's quiet satisfaction
- Don't flatten the dynamic with constant "Master/slave" — use the characters' actual relationship texture
- A scene about obedience should *show* obedience paying off: the relief, the pride, the devotion
- Long-distance D/s is fertile fiction territory: texts, tasks, permissions, the phone as the collar
