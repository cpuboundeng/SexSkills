# Foot Worship Guide

This reference covers foot play, worship techniques, and trampling for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

Foot worship centers erotic devotion on the feet — kissing, licking, massaging, and adoring them as objects of desire and symbols of submission. It's a natural fit for D/s: the sub at the Dom's feet is already in the position of worship, and the feet — the lowest part of the body — make the hierarchy physical. The foot's sensitivity, the intimacy of tending another person's body, and the devotion of mouth-to-foot contact make it one of the most accessible and ritual-friendly kinks.

## Worship Techniques

- **Kissing**: toes, arch, sole, ankles — each zone has a different intimacy
- **Licking**: slow, deliberate strokes of the sole; between toes; the arch — worship as tasting
- **Sucking**: toes taken into the mouth — the deepest devotion gesture
- **Massaging**: firm, oiled, attentive — care as worship
- **Blowing**: breath across the sole or between toes — teasing sensitivity
- **Nail play**: kissing, biting, or painting toenails — meticulous attention
- **Through fabric**: worshipping through stockings or socks — the texture layer
- **The ritual form**: feet washed, dried, oiled, then worshipped — devotion made ceremony

## Trampling Play

| Type | Description | Intensity |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| **Standing** | Dom stands on the sub's back, chest, or thighs | Light-Medium |
| **Walking** | Dom walks across the sub's body | Medium |
| **Guided** | The Dom directs the sub by stepping where they indicate | Medium |
| **Weight play** | The sub holds the Dom's full weight on a body part | Hard |
| **Foot-on-face** | Foot placed on the face as dominance | High-sensitivity; careful |

**Trampling safety**: target fleshy, supported areas (back, thighs, chest with care); never the neck, throat, head, joints, or stomach; the Dom must have a stable support (wall, frame) and the sub must be on a firm, even surface; communicate weight tolerance in real time.

## Scene Ideas

- Trampling: the sub on the floor, the Dom stepping with deliberate weight
- After-scene worship: the Dom's feet washed and kissed as the closing ritual

## Safety & Considerations

- **Hygiene**: feet carry odor and sweat — some people love this, others need fresh feet; confirm comfort levels
- **Skin**: check for cuts, athlete's foot, or broken skin before mouth contact; check the worshipper's mouth health too
- **Toenails**: sharp or rough nails can cut the mouth — file or be careful
- **Products**: lotions, creams, and nail polish can be irritants or toxic in the mouth — confirm before licking
- **Trampling**: see above — weight on the wrong area is genuinely dangerous
- **The intimacy note**: foot worship can be more vulnerable than it looks — the sub's devotion is naked; treat it with care
- **Aftercare**: feet can be hyper-sensitive after worship; moisturize, warm them, check in

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Assuming everyone wants foot contact | Feet are a hard limit for many | Ask, always |
| Ignoring hygiene concerns | Soreness, infection, discomfort | Clean, check, confirm |

| Rushing the ritual | The devotion IS the kink | Slow, deliberate, ceremonial |
| Treating it as "lesser" play | It's a core scene for many | Give it the weight it has for the players |

## Writing Tips

- The devotion is the erotic content: the slowness, the reverence, the sub's mouth on the lowest part of the body as the highest act of worship
- Sensory detail carries it: the warmth of skin, the arch of the foot under a tongue, the texture of stockings, the smell of clean skin and leather
- The hierarchy is visual: the Dom seated, elevated, the sub kneeling below — describe the geometry of submission
- The ritual form writes itself: washing, oiling, kissing — each step of the ceremony deepening the charge
