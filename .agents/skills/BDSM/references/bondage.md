# Bondage Guide

This reference covers bondage types, positions, safety, and scene structure for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

Bondage is the consensual restraint of a partner. Physically it uses cuffs, rope, tape, or furniture to restrict movement; psychologically it creates the experience of surrender made tangible — the bound person *feels* their lack of control in the pressure of the restraint, the bound person's trust in the *giver* of that trust. Done well, bondage is as much about the mind as the rope: the slow breath when the bound person stops fighting the restraint is the moment the scene turns.

## The Two-Finger Rule & Nerve Safety

- **Two-finger rule**: two fingers fit between restraint and skin. Never tighter.
- **Never cross with pressure**: joints and hollows carry nerves close to the surface.
- **Danger zones**: inner wrists and forearms (median/ulnar/radial nerves), inner elbows, armpits (brachial plexus), backs of knees (peroneal nerve), inner thighs/groin (femoral nerve), sides of the neck.
- **Signs of nerve trouble**: tingling, numbness, coldness, "pins and needles," loss of strength. The instant a bound person reports these, release that point.
- **Safe areas for pressure**: fleshy zones — forearms' outer face, upper arms, thighs, calves, hips, chest (watch breathing).

## Safety Rules

1. **EMT shears within reach** — always, before the first knot. Never plain scissors (can't cut rope under tension safely against skin).
2. **Never leave the bound person unattended** — not "for a moment," not "to get water."
3. **Time limits**: 15–20 minutes per area for beginners; rotate or release. Ties that put weight on nerves are shorter still.
4. **Circulation checks**: color, warmth, and sensation in fingers/toes every few minutes.
5. **Position checks**: muscle cramping, joint strain, breathing restriction (chest ties).
6. **Don't bind over clothing with seams or pressure points; check for jewelry.**
7. **Warn about marks**: rope leaves impressions and sometimes bruises; confirm the bottom is okay with marks.
8. **Aftercare**: unbind slowly, massage the marked areas, check skin, warmth, water, emotional check-in.

## What It Feels Like

- **The moment of restraint**: a shiver of surrender — the body understands before the mind does
- **The settling**: the deep breath when the bound person stops resisting and sinks into the rope
- **The pressure**: warm, encompassing, like being held by the whole body rather than hands
- **The fight-or-flight flicker**: brief panic when movement is discovered impossible — and the choice to breathe through it is itself the submission
- **Marks**: rope impressions that fade, a blush of reddened skin that lingers like a memory

## Scene Arc

1. **Setup** — the space, the rope/cuffs laid out, the shears in reach, the safeword confirmed
2. **First touch** — slow application of the first restraint; the bound person's breath changes
3. **Building** — adding points of restraint, testing give, letting them feel each new loss of freedom
4. **The settle** — the moment the body accepts the tie; this is the scene's heart
5. **Play within the tie** — impact, sensation, teasing, orgasm control, worship
6. **Release** — slow, deliberate unbinding; skin rediscovering freedom; often more intimate than the tying
7. **Aftercare** — massage the marks, warmth, water, check-in

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Dangerous | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Restraints too tight | Circulation/nerve damage | Two-finger rule, always |
| Rope over joints/neck | Nerve compression, worst-case permanent damage | Learn the nerve map before tying |
| No quick release | Emergency can't be answered | EMT shears in reach, always |
| Leaving the scene | Bound person can't self-rescue | Never unattended, ever |
| Tying on the bed edge / unstable furniture | Falls | Test the anchor points |
| Skipping the check-in | Numbness goes unnoticed | Ask about fingers/toes regularly |
| Tightening "just a little more" | The last twist is where damage happens | Tight enough is enough |
| Forgetting aftercare | Marks and emotional residue untreated | Massage, warmth, check-in |

## Writing Tips

- The erotic detail is in the *specifics*: the click of a cuff, the rough slide of jute, the moment a bound wrist is tested against the knot and finds no give
- Describe the bound person's interiority: the brief panic, the breath, the choice to trust
- The Dom's hands on the rope are a character — deliberate, patient, possessive
- Marks are dialogue: reddened skin that says *held*, impressions that fade like a promise
