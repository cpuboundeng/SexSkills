# Service Worship Guide

This reference covers service types, worship techniques, and ritualized devotion for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

Service worship is submission expressed through devoted acts of care: the sub serves the Dom — massaging, bathing, feeding, dressing, cleaning, kneeling at their feet — not as chores but as worship. The *attitude* is the kink: a service sub doesn't just bring coffee, they bring it as an act of devotion, in a posture of reverence, at a moment of the Dom's choosing. The Dom's comfort becomes the sub's purpose, and the sub's fulfillment comes from being useful to the person they belong to.

## Types of Service

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Physical service** | Massages, bathing, grooming, foot care, body worship |
| **Domestic service** | Cooking, cleaning, laundry — done to the Dom's exact preferences |
| **Personal service** | Dressing the Dom, brushing hair, polishing shoes, packing bags |
| **Feeding service** | Preparing and serving food; feeding the Dom directly |
| **Ritual service** | Daily rituals — morning coffee, evening tea, the nightly report |
| **Verbal service** | Praise, gratitude, affirmation directed at the Dom |
| **Errand service** | Tasks performed outside the home — errands, shopping, admin |
| **Total service** | The sub's routine structured around serving the Dom |

## Worship Techniques

| Technique | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Kneeling service** | Serving from the knees — posture of devotion |
| **Floor service** | Serving from below — the Dom's feet on the sub's back |
| **Silent service** | Serving without speech unless spoken to |
| **Ritualized service** | A fixed order, fixed words, fixed methods — ceremony in repetition |
| **Service as worship** | Each act performed with reverence — the act is a prayer |
| **Service with presence** | The sub present but invisible — anticipation of needs before they're spoken |
| **Praise-fed service** | The Dom's acknowledgment as fuel — "good sub" landing like sunlight |

## The Service Scene

1. **The frame** — what service today requires; the Dom's instructions
2. **The preparation** — the sub readying the space, themselves, the tools
3. **The serving** — the acts themselves, performed with devotion; the Dom receiving
4. **The acknowledgment** — the Dom's appreciation, the praise, the touch
5. **The release** — the sub released from service, restored to partner
6. **Aftercare** — service can be emotionally deep; the sub may feel fulfilled or drained — check in

## Scene Ideas

- The sub kneeling at the Dom's feet, massaging them while the Dom reads
- Morning ritual: the sub brings the Dom's coffee exactly as they like it, kneeling
- Bath service: the sub bathing the Dom — washing hair, rinsing, drying
- Shoe polish: the sub polishing the Dom's shoes while the Dom talks, the sub listening
- Dinner service: the sub serving the Dom dinner from the floor, eating only what's given
- Grooming: brushing the Dom's hair, trimming nails, applying oil
- The nightly report: the sub recounting their day at the Dom's feet

## Safety & Considerations

- **Boundaries**: some service is intimate (bathing, grooming) — clarify what's allowed and what's uncomfortable
- **The meaning differs**: service can feel worshipful or degrading depending on the person and context — negotiate the flavor
- **Physical strain**: kneeling, floor service, and long sessions strain knees, backs, and wrists — pads, breaks, position changes
- **Not just chores**: the worship is in the attitude, not the task — a resentful sub doing tasks is a broken dynamic
- **Balance**: some subs love constant service; others find it exhausting — service levels are negotiated, not assumed
- **Recognition**: Doms must acknowledge and appreciate service — gratitude is part of the exchange, not optional
- **Aftercare**: service subs can feel deeply fulfilled after good service — or empty if it was taken for granted; the Dom's acknowledgment is the aftercare
- **The Dom's responsibility**: receiving service well is a skill — the Dom must be present, appreciative, and attentive to the sub's energy

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Treating service as free labor | Resentment builds | Appreciation and reciprocity |
| Assuming all service is sexual | Much service is simply devotion | Negotiate the sexual element |
| No boundaries negotiated | Intimate tasks cross lines | Clarify what service means |
| Unacknowledged service | The sub's gift is ignored | Praise and acknowledgment |
| Over-servicing | Exhaustion, resentment | Balance; the sub can say no |

## Writing Tips

- The devotion is the erotic content: describe the sub's care, the posture of worship, the joy in being useful
- The Dom's acknowledgment is the payoff beat: the touch, the praise, the "good sub" that makes the service mean something
- Ritual detail builds the scene: the exact way the coffee is made, the kneeling, the order of the bath — ceremony is intimacy
- The service scenes write beautifully slow: unhurried acts of care that deepen the bond with every gesture
