# Sensory Deprivation Guide

This reference covers deprivation types, hoods, layering, re-entry, and safety for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

Sensory deprivation removes or reduces senses to amplify the rest. Take away sight and touch becomes enormous; take away hearing and the room's presence deepens. Deprivation creates vulnerability — the bottom can't see what's coming, can't track the Dom's hands, can only feel. It pairs with nearly everything: bondage, impact, wax, vibrators, or just the exquisite torture of waiting.

## Deprivation Types

| Type | What It Removes | Effect |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| **Blindfold** | Sight | Touch amplified; anticipation becomes unbearable |
| **Hood** | Sight, partially hearing, identity | Confinement and anonymity; the bottom becomes "just a body" |
| **Earplugs/headphones** | Hearing | Disorientation; the world goes muffled; vibration and touch lead |
| **Mittens/gloves** | Touch in the hands | Can't feel or prepare; helplessness in the hands |
| **Gag** | Speech, taste | Voice removed — the bottom can only respond with body |
| **Total deprivation** | Most senses | Near-total isolation; every remaining sensation is enormous |

## Hood Types

| Hood | Character |
|------|-----------|
| **PVC hood** | Rigid, sleek, limited vision; sci-fi anonymity |
| **Latex hood** | Conforming, shiny, tight; claustrophobic potential |
| **Leather hood** | Structured, classic; often ear flaps and openings |
| **Neoprene hood** | Soft, flexible; more comfortable for longer wear |
| **Mask/half-hood** | Mouth and neck free; breathability, kissing possible |
| **Full sensory hood** | Sealed for maximum deprivation |

## Techniques

- **Layering**: blindfold + earplugs + hood stacks deprivation; add each layer slowly so the bottom can adjust
- **Random touch**: the Dom touches unpredictably — the bottom can't prepare, so every touch lands fresh
- **Sensory alternation**: ice, feather, heat, fingers on the same spot in sequence
- **Forced waiting**: the bottom in darkness, no sound, no clock — the Dom's return is unknown; the waiting is the play
- **Sound play**: whispers, footsteps, fabric sounds the bottom can't place
- **Touch escalation**: light to firm, gentle to intense — the bottom tracking the Dom's hands through skin alone

## What It Feels Like

- **The shift**: the world dimming; the body's other senses waking up one by one
- **The anticipation**: not knowing where the next touch lands — every nerve is listening
- **The float**: prolonged deprivation can produce a meditative, dreamlike state — the bottom adrift in sensation
- **The re-entry**: senses returning in a rush — light too bright, sound too loud, the world suddenly *there* again

## Scene Ideas

- Blindfolded and gagged; the Dom taps body parts, the bottom guesses where
- Hooded with earplugs; the Dom feeds different foods, the bottom identifies by taste and texture
- Blindfolded with mittens; wax drips the bottom can't see coming
- Total deprivation with a flogger — every strike a surprise
- Blindfolded and bound; the Dom strokes, then strikes, the bottom can't tell which is next

## Safety

- **Breathing**: hoods must leave nose/mouth clear; test before sealing; never a hood that seals the airway
- **Heat**: full hoods trap heat — ventilation breaks, monitoring, time limits
- **Anxiety/claustrophobia**: panic can build silently in deprivation; the bottom's ability to signal and be released instantly is sacred
- **Circulation**: hood straps and ties must not restrict blood flow to the head/neck
- **Never leave a deprived person alone** — they cannot see or hear an emergency
- **Sweat and skin**: latex/vinyl trap sweat; wipe faces, allow breaks, watch for irritation
- **Emergency exit**: a hood must be removable in seconds — no knots that jam, no zippers that stick
- **Re-entry care**: bring senses back gradually — remove layers one at a time, dim light, soft voice; the re-entry is as delicate as the deprivation
- **Time limits**: 15–20 minutes for total deprivation; less for first-timers

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Dangerous | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Hood sealed over mouth/nose | Airway blocked | Test breathing; ventilation openings |
| Layering too fast | Panic | Add layers slowly, checking each |
| No non-verbal signal | Can't speak under gag | Tap pattern, object drop, hand squeeze |
| Untended | Cannot sense danger | Never alone, ever |
| Abrupt re-entry | Overwhelm, panic | Remove layers gradually, soft light |
| Ignoring claustrophobia signs | Genuine distress | Release instantly; no "just a few more minutes" |

## Writing Tips

- Deprivation is the ultimate interiority setup: the reader lives inside the bottom's skin, feeling what they feel, never seeing what the Dom does
- The Dom becomes a voice and a touch — describe them through what the bottom can sense: the sound of movement, the warmth of approach, the hands that appear without warning
- Anticipation is the engine: the bottom waiting, listening, feeling their own body become the whole world
- Re-entry is an underrated scene beat: the light flooding in, the Dom's face appearing, the ground returning under the bottom's feet
