# Spanking & Paddling Guide

This reference covers hand spanking, paddles, positions, techniques, and safety for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

Spanking — impact with the open hand — is the most common entry point into impact play and often the entire practice. It's intimate: the top's own body is the implement, the sound is distinctive, the handprint is the mark. Paddling substitutes a rigid or semi-rigid implement for the hand, trading the intimacy of skin-on-skin for deeper thud and broader surface. Both sit at the heart of discipline scenes, brat taming, and punishment dynamics.

## Hand Spanking

### Technique
- **Position**: the bottom over a lap, over a bed/bench, or bent over furniture; the ass is the target — the most padded, safest impact zone on the body
- **The hand**: cupped slightly so the palm and fingers land together (a flat stiff hand produces a sharper slap; a cupped hand a deeper thud)
- **Warm-up**: start with gentle stroking and light pats; build into the first real swats — the warm-up *is* part of the erotic arc
- **Rhythm**: steady, deliberate, building; the pause between swats is as erotic as the strike
- **Placement variety**: alternate cheeks, target the lower curve where the thighs meet the ass (the "sit spot" — intense but safe), avoid the tailbone
- **The follow-through**: a hand pulled back immediately stings more; a hand that lingers after the strike feels possessive and warm

### The Sound
The sound of a spank is part of the scene: the sharp *crack* of a flat hand, the deeper *thwack* of a cupped one, the ass jiggling on impact. Sound builds anticipation before the scene even starts — a single loud spank in a quiet room says everything.

## Paddles

| Type | Material | Sensation |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **Silicone paddle** | Flexible rubber | Thuddy, stingy, "splat" sound |
| **Wooden paddle** | Solid wood | Deep, heavy thud; reddening |
| **Leather paddle** | Stiff leather | Crisp sting with some thud |
| **Perforated paddle** | Wood/leather with holes | Concentrated sting, sharp |
| **Wide paddle** | Broad surface | Spreads impact, less intense per square inch |
| **Spatula** | Wood/plastic | Stingy, precise, playful |

Paddle technique mirrors spanking: warm up, aim for the fleshy sit spot and upper thighs, avoid the tailbone and lower spine. A heavy paddle needs fewer strikes than a hand — the impact is deeper.

## Positions

| Position | Setup | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| **Over the lap** | Bottom draped across thighs | Classic; intimate; face and reaction visible |
| **Over the bed** | Bent over mattress, hips raised | Accessible; good for discipline scenes |
| **Holding position** | Hands on ankles, standing bent | Bottom holds the pose; release = swat continues |

| **Bound** | Restrained, then spanked | Helplessness compounds the sensation |

## Marks

- **Handprints**: the hand mark blooms red, fades to pink over minutes, sometimes a faint imprint for hours
- **Reddening**: uniform warmth — the classic "red ass" after a good session
- **Bruising**: heavy repeated impact can bruise; negotiate mark tolerance before play
- **The sitting ache**: a well-spanked ass aches when seated for hours — a delicious reminder that persists

## Discipline Use

- **Corner + spanking**: the spanking, then corner time to sit with it — the punishment arc
- **Counting**: the bottom counts each swat and thanks the Dom — ritual, control, and gratitude intertwined
- **Bratting payoff**: the brat earns the spanking they were asking for — see `dominance_submission.md`
- **Spanking as reward**: some dynamics use it as the reward itself; both are valid if negotiated

## Safety

- **Target zones**: the fleshy center and lower curve of the buttocks, upper thighs
- **Avoid**: tailbone, lower spine, kidneys, the backs of the knees
- **Same spot rule**: 3–4 strikes max on one spot before moving
- **Warm up always** — cold spanking is the difference between pleasure and injury
- **Marks check**: ask about mark tolerance; visible marks have real-world consequences
- **The bottom's signals**: tensing, breath-holding, or going quiet can mean overload — check in
- **Aftercare**: cool compress or warm touch, gentle massage, water; the ass may sting when sitting afterward

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Striking the tailbone or lower spine | Genuine injury risk | Target the fleshy sit spot and cheeks |
| Too hard, too soon | Shock and bruising | Warm up; build intensity in waves |
| Handprints without consent | Marks have real-world consequences | Confirm mark tolerance |
| Same spot repeatedly | Overheating, bruising | Alternate cheeks and zones |
| Ignoring the bottom's signals | Tensing and quiet can mean overload | Check in; watch the body |
| Paddle misjudged as "a hand" | Paddles hit far harder than palms | Fewer strikes; heavier calibration |

## Scene Arc

1. **Set the frame** — why the spanking happens (discipline, bratting, pure pleasure)
2. **Position** — the bottom placed; the first exposure of the target
3. **Warm-up** — stroking, light pats; the bottom's body relaxing into it
4. **Build** — rhythm and intensity rising; sounds and reactions compounding
5. **The peak** — the hardest sequence; often the point of surrender or tears
6. **Aftermath** — the bottom held over the lap, the sting glowing; tenderness replacing intensity
7. **Aftercare** — the reddened skin cared for; the emotional landing

## Writing Tips

- The sound and the reaction are the scene: the *crack*, the gasp, the jiggle, the growing redness
- Count out the swats in dialogue — the ritual is inherently erotic
- The Dom's hand lingering after the strike, the warmth of the palm on hot skin, is the intimate detail readers feel
- Describe the ache that lingers — sitting down later, wincing with a smile — the mark that keeps the scene alive after it ends
