# Fetish Play Guide

This reference covers material, object, and body fetishes and how to weave them into scenes for the BDSM skill.

## What It Is

A fetish is an erotic focus on a specific object, material, body part, or situation — the thing itself becomes the source of arousal, not the means to it. In BDSM scenes, fetishes provide texture and meaning: the sub dressed in latex isn't wearing a costume, they're *in* their desire. Fetish play is about sensory immersion — the smell of leather, the slickness of rubber, the authority of a uniform — and about the power of the object to transform the wearer and the viewer.

| **Food/food play** | Texture, feeding, submission | Feeding scenes, tasting, the mess as surrender |

## Integrating Fetish into Scenes

- **The fetish as ritual**: the dressing scene is the beginning — the sub putting on the latex, the Dom supervising, the transformation happening in front of their eyes
- **The fetish as power object**: the Dom's leathers or boots are worn *as authority*; the sub's fetish items are worn *as submission*
- **The sensory layer**: the fetish object adds constant sensation — the pressure of latex, the smell of leather, the sound of nylon — so the scene is always touching the kink
- **The reveal**: unveiling the fetish object (the uniform appearing, the collar being produced) is a scene beat of its own
- **Fetish + D/s**: the Dom controls the fetish — when the sub may wear it, what they may do in it, who may see

## What It Feels Like

- **For the wearer**: the material becomes a second skin — latex's enclosing pressure, leather's weight, nylon's glide; the arousal is in the *being dressed*
- **For the viewer**: the object transforms the person — a uniform makes a person an authority; latex makes a body a sculpture
- **The scent layer**: every material has a smell — leather, rubber, powder, starch — and smell is the most primal sense; describe it
- **The identity shift**: fetish wear changes how people feel and move — the posture of heels, the stillness of latex, the formality of a uniform

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | Fix |
|---------|---------------|-----|
| Treating a fetish as a costume | Misses the point — the object IS the arousal | Center the fetish; let it lead the scene |
| Latex/PVC allergies ignored | Rash, breathing trouble | Test materials; keep alternatives |
| Overheating in tight materials | Heat exhaustion | Time limits, hydration, breaks |
| Assuming the fetish's meaning | One person's leather is another's uniform | Negotiate what the object means |
| Restrictive gear + demanding play | Balance and safety risk | Extra care when combining gear with impact/bondage |
| No removal ritual | The return to personhood gets skipped | The undressing is part of aftercare |

## Scene Ideas

- A latex-clad sub being inspected, posed, and used like an object
- The Dom in full leather, the ritual of donning the gear, the sub kneeling as each piece appears
- A uniform scene: the nurse's authority flipping into the scene's power structure
- Stocking worship: the sub's legs in nylon being worshipped through the fabric
- Gag as the scene's centerpiece: the gag being chosen, the sound it takes, the drool it collects
- The gear laid out on the bed like an altar — the anticipation of each piece being applied

## Safety & Considerations

- **Material sensitivities**: latex allergies are real; PVC and certain rubbers irritate some skin — test before scenes, have alternatives
- **Heat**: latex and PVC trap heat badly — time limits, hydration, breaks
- **Movement**: heels, muzzles, and restrictive clothing change movement and balance — extra care in scenes with impact or bondage
- **Cleanliness**: latex and leather need proper care; shared items need cleaning
- **The fetish is personal**: what's a fetish for one person is just clothing for another — negotiate its meaning, don't assume
- **Aftercare**: fetish scenes end with the object's removal — the undressing is part of the return to personhood

## Scene Arc

1. **The opening move** — the first roughness: the grip, the hair yank, the pin
2. **Escalation** — intensity building through force, speed, and words
3. **The peak** — the hardest, deepest, most forceful passage
4. **The turn** — a sudden gentleness: the soft touch after force, the kiss after the grip
5. **The finish** — release, collapse, the held moment
6. **Aftercare** — marks traced, the check-in, the landing — see the `aftercare` skill

## Writing Tips

- Fetish writing lives in sensory detail: the *smell* of leather, the *sound* of latex shifting, the *weight* of a boot on the floor, the *sight* of a body transformed
- The dressing ritual is a scene — describe each piece, the transformation, the growing charge in the room
- The fetish object is a character: it has presence, voice (the creak, the click, the swish), and meaning
- Show the wearer's interiority: the shift that happens when the latex zips up, the heels go on, the uniform buttons — the person becoming the fetish
