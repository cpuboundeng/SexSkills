# VERBAL EROTICA

## Overview

Verbal erotica is arousal through **words** — the spoken and the sent. It spans two mediums that share one craft (language as the sex organ) but differ in *where* the words land:

| Medium | The words happen | The engine |
|--------|------------------|------------|
| **Dirty talk** | In the moment, to a present partner | Tone, command, the psychology of voice |
| **Sexting** | Across a distance, to an imagined partner | Pacing, implication, the gap of waiting |

Both are deeply underused in smut: a single well-placed sentence can out-work a paragraph of physical description, because words go straight to the mind — and the mind is the largest sex organ.

**Key distinction:** Dirty talk is *not* sexting typed out. Dirty talk lands on a partner who is *right there*, and its craft is tone, register, and the power exchange in the voice. Sexting lands on a partner who is *absent*, and its craft is the rhythm of the exchange — the typing bubble, the gap, the imagination filling in. One skill, two mediums; route below.

## The Shared Psychology

- **Validation** — hearing (or reading) "I want you" activates the same reward circuitry as touch.
- **Power exchange** — commands and titles ("good girl," "sir," "please") assign roles in an instant. Voice is the cheapest, most portable kink implement.
- **Taboo** — explicit language breaks the "polite" register; the contrast between public manners and private filth is the engine.
- **Anticipation** — "wait until I get you home" / "when I get home…" builds arousal before any touch.
- **Reduced inhibition** — speaking desire lowers the guard and deepens vulnerability; the more someone says, the more present they are.

## The Two Registers at a Glance

### Dirty talk (spoken, present partner)
Praise, degradation, commands, questions, narration, begging, pure sound. The craft is **tone** — matching the voice to the character and the power dynamic.

### Sexting (text/audio, distant partner)
The thread, the photo, the voice note, the phone call. The craft is **pacing** — turn-by-turn escalation, the deliberate gap, the tease of what's *not* said.

## Voice as Character

The most important craft rule in both mediums: *what would this person actually say?* A buttoned-up character's dirty talk is halting, shocked at their own words; a confident Dom's is smooth and assured. The moment a polite character breaks into filth is the scene's turn — make that register shift deliberate.

Keep it **short**. Real erotic talk is terse. A breath carries more than a sentence. Interrupt dialogue with body language — a gasp swallowing a word, a kiss ending a sentence.

## Consent & Frame

Degradation and commands read as abuse without an agreed context. The structure — a safeword, a nod, an enthusiastic "yes," an established sexting thread — is part of the eroticism, not a break in it. Aftercare matters when the words have teeth.

## Scene Structure Skeleton

1. **The opening** — the first words (spoken low, or a message that opens a door).
2. **The escalation** — questions become commands; hints become invitations.
3. **The break / the gap** — a word caught in the throat; a typing bubble that appears and stops.
4. **The turn** — the register shift, or the photo/voice note that tips playful into hungry.
5. **The peak** — "Come for me. Now." / the heat resolving across the distance.
6. **The landing** — praise replacing command; the soft "goodnight" that means more.
